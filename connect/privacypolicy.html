Privacy Policy for Connect

Last updated: September 25, 2026

Connect is an offline visual planning app for mapping how your emails, accounts, finances, people, and tasks connect, using blocks and lines on a board.

Connect is developed by Silver Compass.

This privacy policy explains how Connect handles data. In short: it doesn't collect any.

Data Collection

Connect does not collect any data. Everything you enter into the app stays on your device. The app does not have internet permission and never sends data anywhere.

Third-Party Services

Connect does not use any third-party services that collect or receive user data. There is no Firebase, Google Analytics, AdMob, Supabase, crash reporting, advertising, or cloud service of any kind integrated into the app.

The app is built using open-source libraries (Capacitor and AndroidX) that run entirely on your device and do not collect or transmit any data.

Accounts

Connect does not have accounts, sign-up, or login. You do not need to provide any personal information to use the app.

Children's Privacy

Connect is not directed at children under 13. Since the app collects no data from anyone, no data is collected from children either.

Data Deletion

Because all data stays on your device and nowhere else, you have full control over deleting it. You can:

  • Delete a board from within the app, using Board options
  • Erase everything through Android Settings > Apps > Connect > Storage > Clear storage
  • Uninstall the app entirely

Changes to This Policy

If this privacy policy is ever updated, the new version will be posted at this same URL with a revised "Last updated" date.
