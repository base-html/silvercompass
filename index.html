Enjoying the app?

A five-star rating on the Google Play Store goes a long way for a small studio. If something's not working right, email us first — silver.compass.dev@proton.me — before leaving a bad review. We'd rather fix it than lose a star over something we didn't know about.

What we handle

Two things, done properly.

Interface design

Every app starts as a concept in Figma, worked through screen by screen before any of it gets built.

App development

Built as a native Android app or a web app, whichever fits the project. That's the scope for now, kept deliberately narrow.

No subscriptions

Free tier

Lifetime purchase

How a project runs

Five stages, start to Play Store, in roughly that order.

  1. 1

    Discover

    A call to understand the app, the people using it, and any platform constraints.

  2. 2

    Design

    Wireframes, then high-fidelity screens covering every state, not just the happy path.

  3. 3

    Build

    Development against the approved designs, checked in as it goes.

  4. 4

    Test

    A device and usability pass to catch what only shows up outside a design file.

  5. 5

    Ship

    Play Store submission, plus support once real users start finding the app.
