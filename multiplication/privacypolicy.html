Privacy Policy for Multiplication

Last updated: September 26, 2026

Multiplication is an offline multiplication practice game.

Multiplication is developed by Silver Compass.

This privacy policy explains how Multiplication handles data. In short: everything stays on your device.

Data Collection

Multiplication does not collect or transmit any data. The app works fully offline and does not have internet permission.

To help decide which questions to show more often, the app keeps track of which questions you've gotten wrong. This is stored locally on your device only, is used solely to adjust which questions come up during practice, and is never sent anywhere. We, the developers, cannot see this data.

Third-Party Services

Multiplication does not use any third-party services that collect or receive user data. There is no analytics, advertising, crash reporting, or cloud service of any kind integrated into the app.

Accounts

Multiplication does not have accounts, sign-up, or login. You do not need to provide any personal information to use the app.

Children's Privacy

Multiplication does not collect data from anyone, including children under 13.

Data Deletion

Because all data stays on your device and nowhere else, you have full control over deleting it. You can:

  • Erase everything through Android Settings > Apps > Multiplication > Storage > Clear storage
  • Uninstall the app entirely

Changes to This Policy

If this privacy policy is ever updated, the new version will be posted at this same URL with a revised "Last updated" date.

Contact

Questions about this privacy policy can be sent to silver.compass.dev@proton.me.
